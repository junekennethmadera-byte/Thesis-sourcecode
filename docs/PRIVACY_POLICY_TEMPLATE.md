# Privacy Notice and Institutional Approval Template

This template must be reviewed and approved by Mabini Colleges before production use. It should identify the data controller, data-protection contact, purpose of thesis archiving, lawful basis/consent, access recipients, retention period, correction/deletion process, security safeguards, and complaint procedure under RA 10173.

The system records account data, manuscript metadata, uploaded documents, processing reports, audit events, consent versions, timestamps, and access-related information. Authors retain the rights and ownership defined by institutional policy and RA 8293; archival permission does not by itself transfer copyright.

Do not publish this template as a final legal policy until the institution has supplied its approved wording, retention period, confidentiality classifications, and contact details.
